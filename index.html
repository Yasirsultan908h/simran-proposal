A LETTER FROM YASIR

Some stories
begin with a
simple add.

This one began on Snapchat, took a few unexpected turns,
and quietly became the most important part of my life.

01 — HOW IT STARTEDSCROLL SLOWLY
01

It started with a Snapchat.

We met there, casually. You removed me quite a few times… and I kept sending requests back. Somehow, you kept adding and removing me too.

Looking back, maybe even the beginning had a little bit of our stubbornness in it.

02

Then, slowly, we talked.

With time, the add-remove game became conversations. Conversations became comfort. And without either of us noticing, a friendship quietly found its place between us.

Some bonds don’t arrive loudly. They simply stay.

03

And somewhere in between…

That friendship started feeling like love. I didn’t plan it, and honestly, I didn’t even realise when it happened.

I only know this now: you have become someone I cannot imagine my days without.

THE QUESTION I HAVE BEEN CARRYING

Simran, will you let this

No perfect words. No dramatic promises.
