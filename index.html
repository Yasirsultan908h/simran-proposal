A SPECIAL MESSAGE FOR MERI JAAN

Mubarak ho,
meri jaan.

Aapne Quran hifz complete kar liya…
ye aapki zindagi ki bohot badi kamyabi hai.

DIL SE LIKHA HUA

Meri jaan, mujhe maaf kar dena.

Jab aapne mujhe bataya tha ke aapne Quran hifz complete kar liya hai, maine us waqt aapki baat ko woh importance nahi di jo deni chahiye thi.

Ab sochta hoon to afsos hota hai, kyunki ye koi aam baat nahi thi. Aapne bohot mushkil safar complete kiya hai—sabr, mehnat aur Allah ke kalam se judi hui ek khoobsurat kamyabi.

Baaki sab ne aapko mubarakbad di, lekin meri mubarakbad shayad der se aa rahi hai. Phir bhi ye dil se hai, aur sirf aapke liye hai.

Aap par mujhe sach mein bohot fakhr hai. 🤍
“

Quran dil mein basa lena
har kisi ke naseeb mein nahi hota,
Allah jise chunta hai,
use apne kalam ka mehmaan banata hai.

— aapki kamyabi ke naam

EK CHHOTA SA TOHFA

Door hoon, lekin
khushi mein aapke saath hoon.

Ye gift meri mubarakbad ka sirf ek chhota sa zariya hai. Asal tohfa to aapki mehnat aur Quran se aapka rishta hai.
