A SPECIAL MESSAGE FOR MERI JAAN

Mubarak ho,
meri jaan.

Aapne Quran hifz complete kar liya…
ye aapki zindagi ki bohot badi kamyabi hai.

DIL SE LIKHA HUA

Aapke liye ek khaas baat.

Meri jaan, jab aapne mujhe bataya tha ke aapne Quran hifz complete kar liya hai, us pal mere dil ne aapko mubarak kehna chaha tha.

Lekin main nahi chahta tha ke meri mubarakbad bhi sabki tarah sirf ek jumla ban kar reh jaye. Main aapke liye kuch aisa khaas karna chahta tha jo aapki is badi kamyabi ke laayak ho—isi liye us waqt khamosh raha.

Der zaroor hui, lekin meri khushi, meri dua aur aap par mera fakhr usi pal se dil mein tha. Aapne jo haasil kiya hai, woh sach mein bohot khoobsurat aur qabil-e-fakhr hai.

Aapki mehnat ko sirf mubarakbad nahi, dil se izzat milni chahiye. 🤍
“

Quran dil mein basa lena
har kisi ke naseeb mein nahi hota,
Allah jise chunta hai,
use apne kalam ka mehmaan banata hai.

— aapki kamyabi ke naam

EK CHHOTA SA TOHFA

Door hoon, lekin
khushi mein aapke saath hoon.

Ye gift meri mubarakbad ka sirf ek chhota sa zariya hai. Asal tohfa to aapki mehnat aur Quran se aapka rishta hai.
