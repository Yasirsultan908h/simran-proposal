A LETTER FROM YASIR

Some stories
begin with a
simple add.

Hamari kahani Snapchat par shuru hui thi…
lekin mujhe nahi pata tha ki ek din tum meri duniya ka itna zaroori hissa ban jaogi.

01 — HOW IT STARTEDSCROLL SLOWLY
01

Ek random si mulaqat.

Hamari mulaqat Snapchat par hui. Shuru mein sab kuch bilkul normal tha… bas ek add request jaisa.

Mujhe tab bilkul idea nahi tha ki ye chhoti si mulaqat meri favourite story ban jayegi.

02

Add. Remove. Phir request.

Tumne mujhe kaafi baar remove kiya, aur main bhi har baar request karta raha. Tum add karti, phir remove karti… aur main phir bhi haar nahi maanta tha.

Shayad hum dono ko tab bhi ek doosre se baat karni thi, bas tareeka thoda funny tha.

03

Phir baatein hone lagi.

Dheere-dheere baat badhi. Pata hi nahi chala kab conversations comfort ban gayi, aur kab hum dono friends ban gaye.

Kuch log zindagi mein entry nahi lete… woh dheere-dheere zaroorat ban jaate hain.

04

Dosti se love tak.

Phir ek din samajh aaya ki ye sirf dosti nahi rahi. Ye love tha. Kab hua, kaise hua—mujhe khud nahi pata.

Bas itna pata hai, Sumoo, ab tum mere liye bahut zaroori ho.

“

Ek request se shuru hui thi kahani,
phir baaton ne dil tak raasta bana liya.
Sumoo, tum Snapchat par mili thi,
aur tum mein kisi ne apna sukoon paa liya.

— with all my heart

THE QUESTION I HAVE BEEN CARRYING

Sumoo, kya tum
meri banogi?

Main koi perfect promise nahi karunga.
Bas itna keh raha hoon: tumhari khushi, meri hamesha priority rahegi.

♥Hamari story ka naya chapter shuru.

Thank you, Sumoo. Tumne Yasir ko sabse khush kar diya.
