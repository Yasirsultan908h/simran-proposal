A MESSAGE FOR MERI JAAN

Mubarak ho,
meri jaan.

Aapne Quran hifz complete kar liya…
aur aaj aapki mehnat ka din hai.

01A NOTE FROM THE HEART

Aapke liye,
kuch khaas.

Meri jaan, jab aapne kaha tha ke aapne Quran hifz complete kar liya hai, us pal mere dil ne aapko mubarak kehna chaha tha. Lekin main chahta tha ke meri mubarakbad bhi sabki tarah sirf ek jumla na ho.

Main aapke liye kuch aisa khaas karna chahta tha jo aapki is badi kamyabi ke laayak ho—kuch aisa jo aapko mehsoos karaye ke aapki mehnat mere liye kitni khaas hai. Isi liye us waqt kuch nahi kaha.

Aaj jo ye chhota sa surprise aapke saamne hai, iske peeche meri poori khushi, dil se nikli dua aur aap par fakhr chhupa hai. Aapne Quran ko sirf hifz nahi kiya—use apne dil mein basa liya hai.

✦Aapki khushi ko celebrate karna
mere liye bhi ek khushi hai.
“

Jis dil mein Quran basa ho,
us dil se roshan kya hoga;
Allah ne aapko chuna hai,
isse khoobsurat tohfa kya hoga.

— aapki kamyabi ke naam

Ye gift meri mubarakbad ka sirf ek chhota sa zariya hai. Asal tohfa to aapki mehnat aur Quran se aapka rishta hai.
